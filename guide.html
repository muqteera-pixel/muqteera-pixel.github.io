SECTION 01
Create a League
⌄
1
Tap the + Button
On the home screen, tap the blue + button at the bottom right corner to open the league creation menu.
2
Choose League Type
Select your format: Regular League (round-robin), Knockout (elimination), or Champions League (groups + knockout).
3
Enter League Name
Type a name for your league e.g. "Street Kings Season 1". This will appear on all standings and exported results.
4
Set Number of Teams
Choose how many teams will participate. Minimum 2, maximum depends on league type. Even numbers recommended for balanced fixtures.
5
Confirm & Create
Tap "Create League" — your league is live instantly and ready for teams to be added.
⚡ Instant Setup ✓ Auto Fixtures 🏆 Multiple Formats
💡
You can create multiple leagues at the same time. Star your favourite league to pin it to the top of your list.
👥
SECTION 02
Add Teams & Players
⌄
1
Open Your League
Tap on your league from the home screen to open it, then navigate to the Teams tab.
2
Add a Team
Tap "Add Team" and enter the team name. You can add as many teams as your league allows.
3
Add Players to Team
Tap on any team → tap "Add Player" → enter player name and position. Repeat for all players.
4
Assign Team Manager
Optionally assign a manager name to each team. This helps identify real-life owners of each team in the tournament.

SECTION 03
Enter Match Scores
⌄
1
Go to Fixtures
Open your league and tap the Fixtures tab. All scheduled matches are listed by matchday.
2
Find the Match
Scroll to find the match you played. Matches are sorted by Matchday — e.g. Matchday 1, Matchday 2.
3
Tap to Enter Score
Tap the match row → enter the Home score and Away score using the number pad.
4
Add Goalscorers (Optional)
After entering the score, you can add which players scored. This updates the top scorers chart automatically.
5
Save the Result
Tap "Save" — standings, points and goal difference update instantly and automatically.
✓ Auto Standings ⚡ Live Update ⚽ Top Scorers
⚠️
Entered a wrong score? Tap the match again and edit the score — standings will recalculate automatically.
📊
SECTION 04
View Standings & Stats
⌄
1
Open Standings Tab
Inside your league, tap the Standings tab to see the full league table sorted by points.
2
Read the Table
Each row shows: Position, Team, Played (P), Won (W), Drawn (D), Lost (L), Goals For (GF), Goals Against (GA), Goal Difference (GD), Points (PTS).
3
Check Top Scorers
Tap the Stats tab to see the top goalscorers leaderboard — updated after every match result.
4
Tiebreaker Rules
If two teams have equal points, the tiebreaker order is: Goal Difference → Goals Scored → Head-to-Head result.

SECTION 05
Champions League Mode
⌄
1
Select Champions League Format
When creating a new league, choose "Champions League" format. Best for 8, 16 or 32 teams.
2
Group Stage
Teams are divided into groups of 4. Each team plays everyone else in their group. Top 2 from each group advance.
3
Knockout Rounds
After groups, teams enter Round of 16 → Quarter Finals → Semi Finals → Final. One loss and you're out!
4
Advance Teams Manually
After all group matches are played, tap "Advance to Knockout" — the app automatically places qualified teams into the bracket.
5
Crown the Champion
When the Final is played and the result saved, the winner screen appears automatically with confetti and trophy! 🏆
🌟 Group Stage ⚡ Knockout 🏆 Grand Final
💡
Champions League mode works best with 8 or 16 teams for perfectly balanced groups and knockout brackets.
⚠️
Make sure all group stage matches are completed before advancing to knockout rounds. Incomplete groups affect bracket seeding.
📤
SECTION 06
Export & Share Results
⌄
1
Find the Export Button
Inside any league, tap the clipboard/share icon at the top right of the screen.
2
Choose What to Export
Select from: Standings Table, Fixtures List, Top Scorers, or Full League Summary.
3
Export as Image
Tap "Export as Image" — the app generates a clean shareable image of your standings or results automatically.
4
Share to WhatsApp / Social
After export, tap "Share" — choose WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, or any app to share results with your group.
5
Copy as Text
Prefer text? Tap "Copy Text" to copy the full standings as formatted text — perfect for WhatsApp groups.
📸 Image Export 📋 Copy Text 📱 Share Anywhere
